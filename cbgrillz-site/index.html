Privacy Policy

We only collect the details you submit through our booking form (name, contact info, order details) to get back to you about your grillz order. We don't sell or share your information with third parties beyond the services we use to run this site (Firebase and Web3Forms) and never store payment details on this site.

Home / Policy

Policy

All grillz are custom made to your individual mould and specification, so orders can't be refunded once production has started. Turnaround times are estimates and may vary depending on order volume. Payment is accepted by cash, bank transfer or PayPal as agreed at booking. If there's an issue with the fit or finish of your grill, message us directly and we'll do our best to make it right. By submitting an enquiry you agree to be contacted about your order via the details you provide.
